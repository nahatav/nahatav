I'm Valmik, an undergraduate student and researcher at [UC San Diego](https://ucsd.edu/) studying Data Science, with a focus on building AI systems that are both powerful and aligned. I'm a research affiliate at [Mass General](https://www.massgeneral.org/) & [Harvard Medical School](https://hms.harvard.edu/) working on clinical AI systems, a member of [MIT AI Alignment](https://aialignment.mit.edu/), and a UCSD Poseidon Fellow. Before that, as a research intern at [Dartmouth Hitchcock Medical Center](https://www.dartmouth-hitchcock.org/) & [Dartmouth Health](https://www.dartmouth-health.org/), I presented on an optimized retrieval system for pathology reports. My work has been recognized by [Apart Research](https://www.apartresearch.com/) & [BlueDot Impact](https://bluedot.org/), [UCSD](https://ucsd.edu/) & [NSF](https://www.nsf.gov/), and the [Milwaukee Bucks](https://www.nba.com/bucks), among others. My research interests are AI × bio and AI safety.
